# Test campaign

Before launch, close the game, generate a preflight, require `safeToInstallNow=true`, install with a verified backup, and retain hashes and commits.

1. Load a new non-tutorial career.
2. Verify UI, wallets, company creation, and starter entitlement.
3. Test purchase, allowed/refused placement, retry, and resale.
4. Test inbound and outbound leases.
5. Complete freight, industrial, and passenger contracts with one payout.
6. Save, return to menu, reload, and replay a command to prove idempotence.
7. Test dissolution and net distribution.
8. Verify exact-asset cleanup protection and no protection for a merchant witness car.
9. Configure tracks and enable strict mode: no new free consists; existing jobs remain cancellable.
10. Test SelfShunt host-only execution, zero payout, retry without duplication, and production resumption.
11. Repeat sensitive operations with a client, two reconnects, and host/client report comparison.

Stop mutations immediately on ownership divergence, duplicate payout, duplicated vehicle, debit without delivery, or host/client disagreement. Preserve the session and evidence before reload.
