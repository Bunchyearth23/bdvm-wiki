# BDVM.Operations

[Repository](https://github.com/Bunchyearth23/bdvm-operations) · 1.2.0 · Requires Common, Companies, and Fleet

Manages freight and industrial contracts, stock, reservations, manifests, loading, unloading, partial delivery, backpressure, and payouts. Its strict vanilla-job gate preserves open jobs and rolls back activation if preservation cannot be proven.
