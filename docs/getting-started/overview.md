# Overview

BDVM replaces license-gated progression with economic progression. Licenses may represent insurance, certification, fees, or deposits, but are not intended to become frustrating hard gates.

Each player has a persistent personal wallet and may remain independent or join one company. Each company has its own wallet, members, permissions, assets, and contracts, starts with zero funds, and is sustained by its members. Rolling stock may be personal, company-owned, purchased, or leased. Operators must supply owned or leased equipment for every contract; stations produce cargo, never wagons.

## Deadlock prevention

Each player receives the default personal starting amount, one DE2, and three wagons exactly once. The entitlement is tombstoned and cannot be recovered through company recreation or reconnects.

On dissolution, contracts are cancelled, vehicles are sold, and debts and penalties are deducted. A positive remainder is divided equally among members. If the result is zero or negative, nothing is distributed and residual debt is cancelled to prevent a deadlock.
