# Rolling stock

Every locomotive, wagon, and passenger car is tracked by `CarGUID`. A company may assign a custom visible name or number, but that label never becomes technical identity.

The final acquisition flow uses a catalog. Purchase reserves finite inventory, debits an authorized account, and grants one free initial placement at a validated depot or service track. Failed physical delivery remains pending for reconciliation; it must never become “money removed without a train” or “train created without payment.”

In strict mode, only starter delivery, purchase, lease, and bounded recovery may create economic rolling stock. AI Traffic remains external. Natural locomotives and contract-provided consists are blocked. Maintenance is manual and organized by players or companies; no AI employee drives or services assets.
