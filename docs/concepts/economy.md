# Economy and companies

Every transaction targets an explicit personal or company account. Transfers are audited, idempotent, and host-validated. A client never submits a balance as truth.

Company creation is free and starts at zero. Membership uses applications and invitations by default, with configurable open joining. The leader delegates sensitive permissions such as withdrawals, purchases, contracts, fleet management, and dissolution.

Rolling stock in perfect condition resells for 50% of its value. The rate decreases linearly with condition to a 15% floor. Reserved, leased, contracted, or physically unresolved assets cannot be sold.

Persistent cooldowns, starter tombstones, transfer provenance, multi-hop cycle detection, frozen quotes, and an idempotent ledger protect the dynamic economy from obvious exploits.
